# Code of conduct

We want iZiDatePicker to be a welcoming project for people of every language, background and experience level (the library itself serves right-to-left and left-to-right users alike).

**Expected:** be respectful and patient; assume good faith; give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the users and the project; credit others' work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing private information, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub (repository Security tab or a direct message to an owner). This text is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
